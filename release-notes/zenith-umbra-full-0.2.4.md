**New**

- **Play of the Game.** Every match ends with the best play, shown as a card and a replay.
- **Kill cam** when you are eliminated.
- **Highlights.** Career > History keeps your best play of every match for 48 hours (10 at most); watch them or save them as video (1080p; MP4 when ffmpeg is installed, AVI otherwise; no sound in the file yet).
- **Online:** seats filled by the AI are named Bot 1, Bot 2 and so on; players show by username.

**Fixed**

- Stadium matches froze after the first round when Raijin's storm giant was on the field.
- Tenkai-Oh now grows into the Dawn Colossus.
- The red flash when you are hit is a soft glow at the screen edges.
- Rows of houses no longer float over the sea on Kagura, Lantern, Hanabi and the Proving Grounds.
- The spectator camera stays out of the Gulch canyon wall.
- The Stadium Armory no longer shows a black block over the health bar.
- Hero select shows the hero at the right size.
- Quitting an online match returns to the lobby without a stale notice, and a failed connection test is no longer remembered.

**Performance**

- On graphics cards with less than 7.5 GB the game now loads 2K textures by default (Options > Video > Texture Quality shows it; choose ULTRA for full 4K). On a 6 GB card a match uses about 1.8 GB of video memory instead of 5.1 GB and goes live about 5 seconds sooner.

**Known**

- Raijin and Enra deal more damage and take less than other heroes in this version.
- Online play in the Full edition is not enabled yet.
