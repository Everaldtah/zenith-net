Everything since 1.3.0, now through the Zenith.net launcher.

- **Online play**: matchmade PvP with anyone who has the game installed. Two players are enough; AI fills the
  other slots. Fast Link netcode adapts to each player's connection.
- **Career Profile**: hours, accuracy, per-hero ratings and match history, Overwatch style.
- **Training Grounds**: Hero Range (spawn any hero as an attacking or defending target, damage meter), ultimate
  charge packs, and the Spar Arena for 1v1 duels against any hero at three difficulties.
- **Engine core**: steadier frame pacing, motion interpolation, GPU-timed dynamic resolution and FSR upscaling.
- **Visuals**: physically based materials, per-map skies and lighting, sharper anti-aliasing.
- **Heroes**: Enra's chain blades, Hayate's ricocheting shuriken, reworked ultimates for Tomoe, Mirei, Raijin,
  Hex, Kaien and Gantetsu, and new maps Sunset Mile and Iron Gulch.
